# AZ Strength Coach

A mobile-friendly 3-day muscle-building and strength routine for adjustable dumbbells, a bench, resistance/stretch bands, and an EZ bar.

## Features

- Guided exercise graphics and form cues
- Three full-body workouts designed for about 35 minutes
- Set, rep, and weight tracking
- Rest timer, progress, history, workout volume, and CSV export
- Workout data saved privately in the browser on your device

## Open the app

https://ahmarzia.github.io/strength-coach/

On a phone, open the link in Safari or Chrome and use **Add to Home Screen** for app-like access.
